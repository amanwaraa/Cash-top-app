CASH TOP R78 — Financial Groups + Integrity

1) Financial Groups
- Built on R77/R75 financial-groups model.
- Closed groups are immutable at the managed storage layer: group-scoped writes/removals are rejected.
- Removed the old read-only banner text from pages.
- Financial groups page uses Cairo and inline SVG folder/lock icons; loading uses a CSS spinner (no missing icon glyphs).
- Opening balances continue to carry cash/banks, inventory, customer receivables and supplier payables.
- Reports read current carried customer/fund balances so opening balances appear in the new group while period sales/expenses remain reset.

2) Fund integrity
- A fund/account with any prior financial movement or non-zero balance cannot be permanently deleted.
- Delete converts used accounts to inactive (soft delete); history and balances stay in reports.
- Central merge protection prevents an import/write from dropping a used fund; it is retained as inactive.
- Inactive funds are hidden from new-transaction selectors (sales, purchases, expenses, customers, suppliers, workers, materials, product purchase and purchase returns).

3) Purchase reversals
- Full purchase deletion validates available stock before reversal.
- Full deletion reverses stock, supplier debt and refunds paid amounts to the original fund(s), including split payments.
- Original supplier/account movements are retained; reversal movements are appended for auditability.
- Deleting one invoice item applies the same stock validation and reverses debt/paid amounts proportionally to the invoice settlement.

4) Cache
- Cache version bumped to R78.
- All 40 HTML pages remain pre-cached.
- Navigation stays Cache-First even when internet is available; background refresh remains enabled.
- Turso bootstrap/usage state keys remain on v77 intentionally to avoid unnecessary full re-bootstrap reads after upgrading.
